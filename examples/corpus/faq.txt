Frequently asked questions

Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Orders above 200 can be paid in three monthly instalments without interest when paying with a credit card on our website. The option appears at checkout.

Do you price match?
We match the price of identical products sold by other shops in the country when the product is in stock at both shops. Price matching does not apply to marketplaces, clearance prices or Black Friday offers.

Can I change my order after placing it?
Orders can be changed or cancelled within one hour of being placed. After that, the warehouse may already have packed the parcel, and the customer must return the items instead.

Do you sell gift cards?
Gift cards are available in values from 20 to 500, as plastic cards in stores or as digital cards by e-mail. They are valid for three years and can be used online and in store.

How do I contact customer support?
Customer support answers by chat and e-mail every day from 9:00 to 21:00. The phone line is open on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00.
