# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy explains how customers of Kestrel & Fern Outdoor Co. can send products back and how money is returned. It applies to purchases made in our stores and on our website.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 45 days of delivery. Members of the Trailmark loyalty program at the Summit tier get an extended window of 90 days. Items bought during the winter clearance event, marked with a red "Final Sale" tag, cannot be returned at all.

Footwear that has been worn outdoors can still be returned if it shows no more wear than a short walk would cause. Our staff decide this at the counter by checking the soles for mud, scratches or worn tread.

## Condition of returned items

Returned products must be clean, dry and complete. Tents must be returned with every pole, stake and guy line that came in the box. Stoves and lanterns must be empty of fuel; we cannot accept any item that still contains gas or liquid fuel, for safety reasons.

## How to start a return

Online orders need a return merchandise authorization number before anything is shipped back. Customers request it from the Orders page of their account, and the number always begins with the letters RMA followed by eight digits, for example RMA-20481937. Parcels that arrive without this number are refused by the warehouse and sent back to the sender.

In-store purchases can simply be brought to any of our shops together with the receipt or the order confirmation e-mail.

## How the money comes back

Refunds are always paid to the original payment method. Card refunds appear on the statement within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse inspects the parcel. Gift card purchases are refunded as a new digital gift card, issued the same day.

The original shipping fee is not refunded, except when the product was defective or we sent the wrong item. In those two cases we also pay for the return label.

## Exchanges

Customers who want a different size or colour can ask for an exchange instead of a refund. We reserve the replacement for 10 days while the original item travels back to us. Exchanges for a different product are handled as a return followed by a new purchase.
