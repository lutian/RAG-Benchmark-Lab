# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options

Standard delivery takes three to five business days and costs 6.90 per order. Orders above 120 ship for free with standard delivery.

Express delivery arrives on the next business day for orders placed before 13:00 local time, and costs 14.50. Orders placed after that time leave the warehouse the following day.

Customers can also choose free pickup in any of our stores. The order is ready within two hours during opening hours, and the shop keeps it for 7 days before cancelling it.

## Where we ship

We deliver to every address in the country, including the islands, although island deliveries take two extra days. International shipping is available to 18 countries in Europe. Duties and import taxes on international orders are paid by the customer when the parcel is delivered.

We do not ship stove fuel canisters by air. Fuel can only travel with standard ground delivery, and it is never sent to the islands.

## Oversized items

Kayaks, canoes and roof boxes are oversized items. They travel with a specialised freight partner, who calls the customer to book a delivery slot. Freight delivery takes 7 to 12 business days and costs a flat 39.

## Tracking and problems

Every order receives a tracking link by e-mail as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. If a parcel shows as delivered but cannot be found, customers should wait 24 hours, check with neighbours, and then contact support, who will open an investigation with the carrier.

Parcels damaged in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the box and of the product.
