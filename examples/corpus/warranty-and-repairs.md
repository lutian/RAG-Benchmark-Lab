# Warranty and Repairs

## Warranty coverage

All products sold by Kestrel & Fern carry a two-year legal warranty against manufacturing defects. Our own brand, Fernline, adds a lifetime guarantee on stitching and zippers for as long as the original buyer owns the product.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage caused by accidents or misuse, or products modified by the owner. Faded colours and worn-out waterproof coatings after years of use are considered normal wear.

## Making a warranty claim

A warranty claim needs proof of purchase and a short description of the problem. Claims are opened at the service desk in any store or through the online support form. The repair workshop in Lisbon decides within 10 business days whether the defect is covered.

If a product is covered, we repair it first. When a repair is not possible, we replace the product with the same model, or with a similar one of equal value if the model is no longer sold. A refund is only offered when neither a repair nor a replacement is possible.

## Paid repairs

Our workshop also repairs products outside the warranty. Common paid repairs and their fixed prices:

- Replacing a jacket zipper: 25
- Patching a tent floor or fly sheet: 18 per patch
- Resoling hiking boots: 55 per pair
- Replacing a broken tent pole section: 12

Paid repairs take about three weeks. Customers receive a quote by e-mail and the work only starts after they accept it.

## Sleeping bags and down products

Down jackets and sleeping bags are cleaned before any repair, at no extra cost, because feathers cannot be sewn safely when they are dirty. This adds one week to the repair time.
