# Product Care Guide

Good care makes outdoor gear last for many seasons. This guide gives the basic rules for our most popular products.

## Waterproof jackets

Wash waterproof jackets at 30 °C with a technical detergent, never with fabric softener, which blocks the pores of the membrane. Close every zipper and the Velcro tabs before washing. After the wash, tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes: gentle heat reactivates the water-repellent treatment on the outer fabric.

When water stops beading on the surface and the fabric looks dark and wet, the treatment is worn out. Restore it with a spray-on proofer, applied to the clean, damp jacket.

## Tents

Never pack a tent away wet. If it must be packed in the rain, set it up again within 24 hours to dry it completely; otherwise mould will stain the fabric and destroy the coating.

Clean tents with lukewarm water and a sponge only. Washing machines tear the seams and the seam tape.

The Fernline Ridgeback 2 tent (product code KF-RB2) uses colour-coded pole clips: the red clips go to the red corners. Its poles should be assembled from the middle outward to avoid stress on the shock cord.

## Sleeping bags

Store sleeping bags loosely in the large cotton storage sack, never compressed in the stuff sack, because long compression damages the insulation. Down sleeping bags can be washed by hand or in a front-loading machine with a special down soap, and must be dried with two or three tennis balls to break up the clumps.

## Hiking boots

Remove the insoles and let boots dry at room temperature, away from radiators and fireplaces. Direct heat cracks the leather and weakens the glue between the sole and the upper. Treat leather boots with wax twice a season.

## Stoves

Clean the burner of the Fernline Blaze stove (product code KF-BLZ) after every trip with the small wire brush included in the box. Store gas canisters in a cool, ventilated place and never inside a tent.
