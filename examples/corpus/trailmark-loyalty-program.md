# Trailmark Loyalty Program

Trailmark is the free loyalty program of Kestrel & Fern. Members collect points on every purchase and unlock benefits as they move up through three tiers.

## Earning points

Members earn one point for every euro spent. Fernline products earn double points. Points are not earned on gift cards, shipping fees or repair services.

Points become available 30 days after the purchase, once the return window for most items has passed. Points expire 24 months after they were earned if the member makes no purchase in that period.

## Tiers

| Tier | Points in the last 12 months | Main benefits |
|------|------------------------------|---------------|
| Base | 0 – 499 | Birthday voucher of 10 |
| Ridge | 500 – 1,499 | Free standard delivery on every order |
| Summit | 1,500 or more | 90-day returns, free express delivery, early access to sales |

The tier is recalculated on the first day of every month based on the points earned in the previous 12 months.

## Redeeming points

Every 200 points can be exchanged for a voucher worth 10. Vouchers are created in the member's account and can be used online or in store within six months. Only one voucher can be used per order.

Points cannot be exchanged for cash, and they cannot be transferred to another member, except between two accounts registered at the same home address.

## Leaving the program

Members can close their account at any time from the Privacy page of their profile. Unused points and vouchers are lost when the account is closed.
