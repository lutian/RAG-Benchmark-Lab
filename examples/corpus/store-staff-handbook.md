# Store Staff Handbook

This handbook is for employees working in Kestrel & Fern stores.

## Opening hours

Stores open from 10:00 to 20:00 from Monday to Saturday and from 11:00 to 18:00 on Sundays. On public holidays each store follows the opening hours of its shopping centre. Staff must arrive 30 minutes before opening to prepare the shop floor.

## Shifts and breaks

A full shift lasts eight hours with a paid break of 30 minutes. Shift swaps must be approved by the store manager at least 48 hours in advance through the scheduling app. During the summer and the December holidays, no more than two people from the same store can be on vacation at the same time.

## Handling returns at the counter

Staff can approve returns up to a value of 300 on their own. Returns above that value need the approval of the shift supervisor. Returned stoves and lanterns must be checked for fuel before being placed in the returns area; any item with fuel goes to the hazardous goods cabinet.

## Rentals

Selected stores rent tents, sleeping bags and climbing equipment. Rentals require a deposit of 100 per item, paid by card. Climbing harnesses and ropes are inspected after every rental and retired after 50 rental days or immediately after any fall that loaded the rope.

## Safety and incidents

Every incident involving a customer or an employee must be written in the incident log before the end of the shift, even when nobody was hurt. The first aid kit is kept behind the main counter and is checked every Monday by the shift supervisor.

In case of fire, staff guide customers to the nearest exit and meet at the assembly point indicated on the evacuation plan next to the staff room.

## Discounts for employees

Employees receive a 30% discount on all products, and 40% on Fernline products, for personal use. The discount cannot be combined with sales promotions or Trailmark vouchers.
